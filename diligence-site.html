Visible limits.

A missing number stays unknown. A scenario stays an assumption. Every source keeps its date and scope.

Public parcel coverage is strongest in Indiana. Zoning and some city data may be unavailable. Cividian supports early investigation; it does not establish entitlements, feasibility, or investment suitability.
