    Using the early-access workspace

    • Check the parcel. An address match or nearby polygon is a candidate, not a survey or a claim of ownership.
    • Use your own assumptions. Defaults are labeled screening conventions. Blank financial fields remain unknown. A missing value is different from zero.
    • Expect gaps. Parcel coverage is strongest in Indiana. Zoning and unconfigured city sources stay unavailable. Calculations and AI interpretation do not establish feasibility.
    • Keep a copy. Guest access lasts up to 24 hours in this browser. Clearing cookies or using another browser loses access. Download JSON or print the HTML before the session ends. Saved-brief URLs are not sharing links.
    • What is processed. Site searches go to the configured public-data providers. Briefs and inputs are stored on this deployment. If AI is enabled, a bounded packet of evidence and assumptions goes to Nebius for NVIDIA model reasoning. Avoid confidential or personal information.
    • Remove your work. Saved briefs has a Remove control. It removes that brief's content from the app; downloads and any provider-side processing are separate.
    • Tell the team what happened. Report an issue downloads a small diagnostic file for you to review and send to the person who invited you. It sends nothing automatically.

    Report an issue

    The report adds the app version, AI availability and brief status. It excludes addresses, financial inputs, cookies and credentials. Your description is included exactly as entered.
